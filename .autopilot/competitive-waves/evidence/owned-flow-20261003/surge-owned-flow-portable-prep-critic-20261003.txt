FOCUSED RE-REVIEW — STABLE OWNED-FLOW CLAIM THROUGH EFFECT LIFETIME
Verdict: ACCEPTABLE at DESIGN level. OF-P25-CRITIC-01 is resolved by the appended amendment; earlier RESHAPE verdict is superseded for this finding only.

Reviewed the complete revised plan including the new claim-lifetime appendix against the prior finding and actual claim/validate_claim call contracts. The FlowLaunch operation/run namespace is fixed and distinct. Its original actual-object/ancestry identity is captured write-once while both Operation and Launch guards remain held before acceptance, so post-acceptance claim acquisition cannot adopt a substituted object as its original. Operation -> Launch is a fixed acquisition order; effect claim acquisition only needs Launch and does not create a reverse Operation acquisition requirement.

Generic claim(run), used by existing launch_attempt and cold lifecycle callers, must route genuine Flow origin to the private stable branch and reject missing/malformed intent/binding; caller JSON cannot select Legacy. The short transaction rechecks accepted origin/revision/workspace, intent, control, generation and actual held identity before changing claim_token. A second live owner cannot steal the original SQL token by acquiring a replacement pathname. validate_claim checks private enum variant against typed origin and persisted operation/run/namespace/identity in addition to SQL ownership. Legacy claim helpers are not reused as owned-flow stable evidence.

Arc shares one lower OS guard rather than cloning/reopening a handle, retains it through daemon and Engine admitted open/prompt work and releases only after the last legitimate holder. Using the lower guard avoids moving StartPreparation's SQL-abandoning destructor into runtime ownership. The explicit state-specific Pending/Reserved first-launch predicate remains separate from Started/continuation/wake authority. Acquisition alone, replay receipt, Empty marker or an Attention/Suspended claim cannot grant an effect. Cancellation and ancestor/object substitution require containment rather than new authority.

The added real effect-owner tests target the seam that preparation-only tests missed: generic claim routing, live lock/ancestor substitution without token mutation or duplicate startup/open/prompt, cold/wake ownership, unchanged-object genuine-death reclaim, changed-object-after-death refusal, malformed-binding refusal and last-Arc cancellation lifetime.
