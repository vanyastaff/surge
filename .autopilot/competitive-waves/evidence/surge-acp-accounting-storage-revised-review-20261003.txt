Resolution of prior REQUIRED reshapes

1. Authority / dedup: resolved. The revision explicitly rejects ALL new authority variants from
AppendEvent, AppendBatch and other generic helpers. Prepared source freezes observation identity;
one authoritative token completion per physical RPC prevents a fresh source occurrence from
charging it again. Separate observation kinds/projector ownership preserve monetary evidence
without duplicate token charging. Semantic/wire replay identity excludes delivery timestamps;
exact replay returns its original result even after baseline advancement/restart, and conflicting
replay rolls back ALL receipt/event/view/baseline/status changes. This preserves the original
atomic conflict invariant, rather than weakening it for raw retention.

2. Overflow / unsigned encoding: resolved. Raw evidence plus explicit Unprojectable status/event
commits atomically while previous totals/baseline remain unchanged. Replay remains idempotent;
conflicts fully roll back. Registry cross-attempt overflow retains evidence/status/cursor and
never exposes a truncated exact total. All new u64 values use strict eight-byte blobs, checked
Rust arithmetic, decimal-string DTOs and exact UI digits. Old INTEGER/REAL/SQL SUM paths remain
historical and are explicitly excluded from new accounting authority. The chosen u64 overflow
policy is clearly stated and does not require a new user decision.

3. Baseline order / ownership / rebuild: resolved. Physical/sample sequences, not arrival order,
control normalization. Late observations remain unprojected; pending settlement prevents another
same-session prompt. Session continuity requires predecessor evidence and an exclusive handoff
fence; string-equal provider IDs cannot transfer historical charges between tasks. Loaded unknown
baselines remain unattributed; interval recovery does not heal turn attribution gaps. Rebuild
uses immutable normalized events and preserves authority tables/baselines without recomputation
or charge creation. Task-triggered auxiliaries include the parent binding and enter totals once;
unrelated and uncertain scopes remain visibly separate.

B. Normalized events must carry immutable error/receipt identity and enough state for deterministic

D. Target-prefix catch-up must not hold an unbounded registry transaction while awaiting a live
   writer. Use bounded pages with exact snapshot/CAS semantics; report the captured prefix and
   future appends separately. This follows the revision's bounded eventual projection contract.
